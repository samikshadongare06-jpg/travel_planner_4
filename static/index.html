We're Voyara — a small student crew obsessed with good days.

Built for wanderers who hate Excel-style itineraries. We turn vibes into a day-wise plan, throw in a budget so nobody overpays, and quietly judge anyone who skips breakfast.

Why we built this

Group trips usually die in the planning chat. We made the planner do the boring math so the chat can stay weird.

How it works

Score → cluster into zones → pack each day with a mix of slow mornings and standout stops. No black-box AI vibes.

Honest budget

Rough numbers, no inflation. We'd rather under-promise than ruin your last day with maggi-only meals.
